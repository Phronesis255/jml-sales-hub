Priorities

    How a live audit is produced

    01 / Intake

    Define the market

    Confirm the business, website, location and primary search query.

    02 / Collect

    Gather evidence

    The private collector measures map-grid ranks, organic results, AI visibility, reviews and technical checks. Provider credentials stay in the private environment.

    03 / Review

    Check the findings

    Review anomalies, separate measured results from expert UX judgment and agree on the most useful fixes.

    04 / Publish

    Share the approved report

    Export a client-ready snapshot after editorial review. The public viewer does not run the collector.
